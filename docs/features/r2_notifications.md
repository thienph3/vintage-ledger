# 🔔 Feature: Notifications (FCM)

## Type
Engagement / Realtime UX

---

## Problem
- Không có notification
- Family feature mất giá trị

---

## Goal
User nhận được update realtime

---

## Solution
Sử dụng Firebase Cloud Messaging

---

## Events

### 1. Family invite
- Khi tạo invite → notify user

---

### 2. New transaction (optional)
- Member khác tạo transaction

---

## Behavior
- Tap notification → mở đúng screen

---

## Scope v1
- Invite notification

---

## Out of scope
- notification settings

---

## Success metric
- Tăng engagement
